THE 4 D’S · THE BELAY LINE
Together We Climb — pick one, do it, move on.
D Say / Do This Use It When
Direct “We don’t do that here.” / “That’s uncalled for.” You’re safe, high-status, or it needs to stop right now.
Distract Ask a question, request help, invite them to walk with you. You want to de-escalate without a scene.
Delegate “Mr. Davis — there’s something happening at locker bank C.” It needs an adult, now or after the fact.
Delay “I saw what happened. That wasn’t okay — you doing alright?” After the moment has passed — check on the target.
It doesn’t matter which D you pick — it matters that you pick one.
